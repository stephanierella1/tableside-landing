How it works

Four steps. Zero Sunday spiral.

01

Build your library

Import from any recipe site, describe something from memory, or add by hand. Your favorite creator just dropped something — it’s in your library in seconds.

02

Shuffle your week

One tap. Smart algorithm, no repeats, varied cuisines — based on what you actually cook and love.

03

List builds itself

Grocery list populates automatically from your plan. Scaled by servings. Categorized. Shared with your household.

04

Cook. Eat. Repeat.

Rate what you made. The app gets smarter. Your household stays in sync. Dinner happens, every night.
